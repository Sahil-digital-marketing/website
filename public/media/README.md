# Homepage eagle footage

`eagle-intro.mp4` is a 2.8-second, muted, color-graded and mirrored edit of
[Head of a Bald Eagle by Mohammed Jabbar on Pexels](https://www.pexels.com/video/head-of-an-bald-eagle-6372980/).
`eagle-poster.webp` is a frame from that clip. Pexels permits free commercial use
and modification without attribution under its [license](https://www.pexels.com/license/).
The edit uses 4.0–6.8 seconds of the source, scales to 1280 × 720, and encodes
H.264 at 24 fps.

the existing `public/brand/sahil-mark.webp` asset.
