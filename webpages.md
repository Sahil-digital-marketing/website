# SAHIL Digital Marketing — Website Plan & Founder Questionnaire

**Website:** https://www.sahildigitalmarketing.com/  
**Research date:** 16 November 2026  
**Purpose:** Agree on website pages, content, positioning and functionality before development.  
**Status:** Proposed scope for founder review; business claims and final content require confirmation.

## 1. Recommendation: how many pages?

Plan for **16 pages at launch**, assuming five confirmed services and two publishable case studies. This is a practical recommendation for this business, not a universal agency requirement or a Google ranking rule.

| Page group | Number | What is included |
| --- | ---: | --- |
| Core business pages | 6 | Home, About, Services, Our Work, Our Process, Contact |
| Individual service pages | 5 | Social Media Marketing, SEO, Google Ads, Content Marketing, Analytics & Reporting |
| Case study detail pages | 2 | Two real, approved client stories |
| Policy pages | 2 | Privacy Policy, Website Terms |
| Conversion utility page | 1 | Thank You after successful enquiry |
| **Total** | **16** | **15 public content/policy pages + 1 utility page** |

**If case studies are unavailable:** launch with 14 pages and use approved work samples on Our Work. Label self-initiated/concept projects clearly. If there are no suitable work samples either, defer Our Work too: 13 pages. Never invent clients or results.

**If Website Development is confirmed as a sixth service:** add one service page, taking the full recommended launch to 17 pages.

**Later:** a blog index plus three useful articles adds four pages, taking the five-service plan from 16 to 20 pages. Add other pages only when there is a clear customer need and someone to maintain them.

## 3. Research findings and reasoning

The recommendation combines the current service list with patterns observed on established agency sites. Larger agencies are structural references, not a requirement to match their scale.

| Research reference | Observed pattern or guidance | Application to SAHIL |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Schbang Services [S2] | Groups its offering into distinct service areas. | Make the service overview easy to scan, with deeper pages for actual offerings. |
| Schbang About and Contact [S3–S4] | Explains the company separately and provides a clear enquiry route. | Give visitors company context and a predictable way to start a project. |
| WebFX Services [S5] | Links to individual services and includes proposal actions. | Connect each service to an enquiry with that service preselected. |
| WebFX Case Studies [S6] | Uses dedicated client stories with outcomes and service context. | Show approved evidence of work rather than relying on generic promises. |
| Google SEO Starter Guide [S7] | Encourages useful, original content, clear titles, descriptive URLs and discoverable links. | Build useful pages around customer questions; page count alone is not an SEO strategy. |
| Google people-first content guidance [S8] | Emphasizes experience, trustworthy information and reader value. | Publish genuine expertise and substantiated results; avoid thin service or location pages. |

**Conclusion:** a compact multi-page website should help prospects understand the offer, assess credibility and enquire. There is no evidence-based “perfect number” of agency pages and no guaranteed first-place Google ranking.

| 16 | Thank You | `/thank-you/` | Confirm successful submission, next steps, alternative contact route | Return Home / Continue Browsing |

**Service-page structure:** customer problem → service scope → deliverables and exclusions → approach → relevant proof → commercial expectations → FAQs → enquiry. Use this consistent structure with unique service-specific content.

**Case-study evidence:** record baseline, final result, measurement period, data source and SAHIL’s exact contribution. Separate paid spend from fees. Avoid presenting correlation as proven causation. Do not publish confidential dashboards.

**Utilities outside the 16-page count:** a designed 404 state, XML sitemap and robots.txt. Use `noindex` for Thank You; confirm success server-side before sending visitors there. A sitemap and robots.txt are technical files, not marketing pages.

## 5. Navigation and visitor journeys

**Header:** Home · Services dropdown · Our Work · About · Our Process · **Start a Project**.

**Footer:** service links, contact details, approved social links, Privacy Policy, Website Terms and copyright. Add Insights only when useful articles are ready.

**Typical journeys:**

1. Search visitor → relevant service → related proof → service-specific enquiry.
2. Referral visitor → Home → About / Our Work → Contact.
3. Campaign visitor → focused landing page, if commissioned → enquiry → confirmed Thank You.

Keep FAQs on the relevant service pages and Home initially. Keep testimonials within Home, About and relevant work pages. These do not need separate pages at launch.

## 6. Optional pages: when to add them

| Page or feature | Add when | Scope impact |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Website Development | Founder confirms it is actively sold and can define scope, support and evidence. | +1 service page |
| Insights / Blog | An author and reviewer can maintain genuinely useful articles. | +1 index and +1 per article |
| Packages / Pricing | Deliverables, starting prices, exclusions and update ownership are approved. | +1 page; otherwise discuss pricing within enquiries |
| Industry pages | SAHIL has distinct expertise, relevant proof and a tailored offer for an industry. | +1 per justified industry |
| Location pages | Real service coverage and useful local information justify each page. | +1 per justified location; avoid copied city-name pages |
| Careers | There are real openings and an application owner. | +1 index; detail pages as needed |
| Campaign landing pages | A specific campaign requires a focused message and conversion path. | +1 per campaign |
| Cookie information / preferences | The actual tools, visitor markets and applicable requirements call for it. | Separate page or integrated policy and controls, as appropriate |

A chatbot, client portal, payments and booking system are separate functionality decisions. They are not prerequisites for a professional agency website.

## 9. Build and launch acceptance checklist

- [ ] Founder has approved services, scope, copy, claims, work permissions and page count.
- [ ] Each service has unique content and an obvious next step; no empty or placeholder pages are public.
- [ ] Mobile and desktop navigation, layouts and forms work; text remains readable without animation.
- [ ] Navigation and forms support keyboard use, visible focus, labels, readable contrast and reduced motion.
- [ ] Every enquiry reaches its destination; success, validation, delivery failures and spam protection are checked.
- [ ] Thank You appears only after confirmed submission, with no personal information in the URL.
- [ ] Conversion events distinguish completed enquiries from clicks and avoid duplicate recording; personal form data is excluded from analytics.
- [ ] Page titles, descriptions, heading structure, internal links, image text alternatives and social previews are reviewed.
- [ ] HTTPS, production indexing settings, canonical URLs, sitemap, robots.txt, redirects and 404 handling are checked.
- [ ] Real images are optimized; performance is checked on mobile, including the intro and other animation.
- [ ] Structured data reflects visible, verified business information and supported types; it is validated without promises of rich results.
- [ ] Policies and any consent controls match actual tools and approved requirements.
- [ ] Account ownership, editing instructions, backups, monitoring and maintenance responsibility are handed over.
